Andreea is an Assistant Professor in Programming Languages at TU Delft. Her research revolves around topics on program verification, automated programming, and program repair, with emphasis on building trustworthy software systems. She is also passionate about how formal methods can strengthen software engineering practices, and how the teaching of programming languages is challenged by the growing use of AI tools for coding.
